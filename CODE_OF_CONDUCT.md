# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be respectful, professional and
free of harassment for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests and reviews.
- Critique work, not people.
- Assume good faith, and ask before assuming.
- Keep discussion on the topic of the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or threats.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces: the repository, its issues and
pull requests, and any channel where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to **info@aimlds.org** with the subject
line `CONDUCT: atom-services`. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and
other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or
permanently block anyone whose behaviour is harmful.
