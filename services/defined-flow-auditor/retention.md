# Retention — defined-flow-auditor

Per-call pricing means retention = being called again. The atom retains by sitting inside a recurring workflow (a gate, a pipeline, a scheduled check), not by lock-in.

**Our own repeat use:** Guardian Mesh audits of our systems — the workflow recurs in our real work, which is the retention pattern external buyers are expected to mirror.

**Churn signal:** trial units issued but not consumed, or a buyer who calls once and never again in 60 days — read at the uptake gates; the response is refine (narrow the service), reprice from measured cost, or retire. Per-atom, never averaged.
