# Retention — creative-ideation-session

Retention is repeat usage of the same metered unit inside the buyer's own recurring work; there is no subscription lock-in by design.

**Our own repeat use:** Content and product ideation — the workflow recurs in our real work, which is the retention pattern external buyers are expected to mirror.

**Churn signal:** trial units issued but not consumed, or a buyer who calls once and never again in 60 days — read at the uptake gates; the response is refine (narrow the service), reprice from measured cost, or retire. Per-atom, never averaged.
